# Marketplace launch copy — draft

Prepared 17 September 2026. Not published.

---

BarTranslate is now available in the official Omarchy plugin marketplace. A native translation panel in your bar, using your current theme, with searchable languages and explicit clipboard paste/copy.

Entered text goes to Google only when you request a translation. The endpoint is unofficial and can change or rate-limit requests.

[Find BarTranslate in the marketplace](https://omarchyplugins.com/plugin.html?id=io.github.tcballard.bartranslate).
